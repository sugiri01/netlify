LMS & HR Administrator

Full-time position for managing learning and human resources systems

Overview

We are seeking a detail-oriented and organized LMS & HR Administrator to join our team. The ideal candidate will have experience in both Learning Management Systems (LMS) and Human Resources administration, with a strong ability to manage multiple tasks and projects simultaneously. This role serves as a critical bridge between our HR department and training initiatives, ensuring efficient management of employee data, training programs, and organizational development.

Key Responsibilities

HR Administration

  • Manage employee records, including hiring documentation, attendance, and leave management
  • Support the recruitment process by coordinating job listings, applications, and interviews
  • Process payroll and maintain confidential HR documentation
  • Ensure compliance with company policies and employment regulations
  • Organize employee performance reviews and maintain performance management data

LMS Administration

  • Handle the day-to-day administration of the company's learning management system
  • Manage course content, create and maintain course catalogs, and set up learning paths
  • Organize batches, manage course libraries, and track training completion rates
  • Coordinate online events, webinars, and domain expert seminars
  • Generate training reports and analyze learning metrics for management review

Additional Duties:

  • Manage document organization systems for both HR and training materials
  • Maintain confidentiality of sensitive employee and company information
  • Coordinate employee recognition programs like "Hack Bees" and "Batch Topers Day"
  • Assist with planning and executing job fairs and placement initiatives
  • Collect payments and handle administrative duties related to training programs
  • Coordinate weekly meetings and assist with meeting documentation
  • Handle petty cash and related administrative expenses
  • Provide policy management support and ensure proper implementation

Weekly Schedule & Time Commitment

  • Weekly HR Meeting: Saturday 1:30 - 2:00 PM (30 minutes)
  • Domain Experts Seminar (Online): Saturday 2:00 - 2:30 PM (30 minutes)
  • LMS Batch Management: Ongoing throughout the week
  • Special Events: Job fairs, awards ceremonies, and recognition days as scheduled

Qualifications & Skills

Required Qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related field
  • Minimum 2 years of experience in HR administration
  • Experience with learning management systems and e-learning platforms
  • Strong organizational and multitasking abilities
  • Excellent attention to detail and record-keeping skills
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)

Desired Skills:

  • Knowledge of HR best practices and employment regulations
  • Experience with HRIS and payroll systems
  • Familiarity with e-learning content development
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Problem-solving abilities and initiative
  • Event coordination experience

Technical Skills & Systems:

HRIS Systems LMS Platforms MS Office Database Management Document Management Basic Web Administration Reporting Tools Video Conferencing Project Management

Documentation & Systems

The LMS & HR Administrator will work with the following documentation and systems:

  • HR Documents: Hiring books, employee databases, JDs, attendance records, leave management, performance reviews
  • Company Documentation: Policy documents, employee handbooks, training materials
  • LMS Resources: Course catalogs, training modules, certification tracking
  • Event Management: Schedules, attendee lists, feedback forms, certificates

Performance Metrics

Success in this role will be measured by:

  • Accuracy and timeliness of HR record management
  • Employee satisfaction with HR services and responsiveness
  • Successful implementation and administration of training programs
  • Training completion rates and effectiveness metrics
  • Efficiency of documentation management systems
  • Successful execution of events and meetings
